## Two concepts to debate (not the rejected strip)

### Concept S — «Два сюжета» (Two stories)
1. **Story 1 — места плана** (short bar or block row, caption «План мест»): shows official plan broken into занято / в общем конкурсе (or БВИ · целевые · вне · общий when counts exist). **No pin. No “ты”. No “выше”.**
2. **Story 2 — дорожка конкурса** (existing rail): only the open contest seats + pin «ты». Label «мест» = openPlan.
3. Gap + different caption + different height so they cannot be read as one queue.

### Concept E — «Вычитание» (Visible subtraction)
Typography/composition equation above the track:

`План 32` − `БВИ 8` − `целевые 0` = `в конкурсе 24`

Then the chance track uses only 24. Optional hairline under the “= 24” that becomes the track width metaphor — but the equation is the primary visual, not a second axis of people.

### Rejected again (do not revive)
- Stacking reserved seats *on* the chance rail with a bridge tick to «ты».
- Full BSU left-column table.
- Overview «БВИ 8» chips.

## What “best” means here
A 17-year-old with score entered, opening биоинженерия, must within ~3 seconds understand:
1. Official plan was 32.
2. 8 seats already left via БВИ (not “8 people beating my score”).
3. I compete for 24.
4. My pin is among score-people for those 24.
